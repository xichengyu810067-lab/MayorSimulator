## 1. Campaign 目標

首波不以公開下載或銷售轉換為目標，而是完成三件事：

1. 讓玩家在十秒內理解「童話城市建造＋可問責治理」的差異。
2. 建立誠實的 Alpha 品牌：已驗證、開發中預覽、路線圖、研究方向分開說。
3. 找出核心社群最想追蹤的內容支柱，作為下一批開發日誌排序依據。

北極星指標：有意義的留言／投票回覆、Discord 核心成員加入、影片 3 秒留存與完整觀看率。現階段不以下載量作 KPI。

## 8. Reddit 深度帖

發文前必須逐一閱讀目標 subreddit 當日規則；不要把同文同步洗到多個社群。Reddit 明確禁止重複或未經邀請的大量互動，並要求以自身產品連結為主的帳號注意發文頻率。

建議先以「尋求設計回饋」而不是「宣傳下載」切入：

**Title**  
`[MVP Alpha] Can a storybook city builder make civic promises mechanically accountable?`

**Body**

> We’re developing **CivicTale: Voices of Promise**, currently at MVP Alpha `0.1.0-alpha.1`. Our core question is whether a city builder can make the player accountable for the promises behind construction.  
>  
> In the verified slice, resident requests persist through reload; healthcare only works when the facility is complete, reachable, maintained and has capacity; vehicles only appear on valid transport networks; and administrative choices can move through council, judicial and oversight systems.  
>  
> The visual direction is a bright storybook 2.5D city, deliberately contrasted with readable budgets and institutional consequences. This is not a public release announcement. Licensing and asset-distribution gates remain open, while large-scale population, deeper family systems, enterprise finance and free-form law processing remain research topics rather than promised features.  
>  
> Which consequence should a mayor feel most strongly: resident trust, fiscal pressure, service access, or institutional checks—and why?

Reddit 不建議堆 hashtag；使用正確 flair、原生上傳圖片／GIF，並在文末透明揭露自己是開發者。
